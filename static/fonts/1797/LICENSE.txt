1797-medium.woff2, 1797-italic.woff2, 1797-poster.woff2 and 1797-signage.woff2 are subsets of
1797-MEDIUM.otf, 1797-ITALIC.otf, 1797-POSTER.otf and 1797-SIGNAGE.otf (Basic Latin, Latin-1
with Spanish letters, typographic quotes and dashes), converted to WOFF2 with fontTools
(pyftsubset --layout-features='*' --flavor=woff2). No other changes. Used only by the admin page
that prints event flyers and draws the Instagram posts (/admin/eventos/agenda-impresa, see
docs/panel.md).
